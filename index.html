About the studio

One developer, two pigs, endless matches

Two Green Pigs is an independent studio of exactly one person: a lifelong gamer who never got over the joy of "just one more match". Somewhere between playing games and taking them apart to see how they tick, playing stopped being enough. So now I make them.

Being tiny is a superpower. There are no committees here: an idea goes from the notebook into the game in days, and every unit, spell and menu gets tested and tuned until it feels right. Feedback does not vanish into a ticket queue either. It lands straight on the desk of the person who makes the game.

And the pigs? They are the studio's mascots: cheerful, a little muddy and always up for a game. Their motto is the studio's promise: oink less, play more.
