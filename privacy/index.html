Privacy Policy

Desert Clash and the Two Green Pigs website · Last updated: 4 October 2026

In short

  • You don't need an account. The game never asks for your e-mail, your real name, your location or your contacts.
  • Online play uses a random player ID and the in-game name you choose. Playing alone sends nothing to us.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell data. Statistics are only collected if you allow them (they are not switched on yet).
  • You can delete your online data in the game (Settings → Privacy → Delete my online data) or write to twogreenpigs.studio@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Two Green Pigs is the name under which Andrei Victor Sircuta, an independent game developer based in Romania, makes and publishes games. Two Green Pigs is not a registered company. The controller of your personal data, in the sense of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), is Andrei Victor Sircuta, Romania.

Contact for anything about your data: twogreenpigs.studio@gmail.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer (the law doesn't require one for a project of this size). Please write to the address above.

"We" and "us" in this policy mean Andrei Victor Sircuta (Two Green Pigs).

2. What this policy covers

It covers the game Desert Clash on every platform where it is offered, including Android and Windows, and test versions given to testers. It also covers this website. If you get the game through a store such as Google Play or Steam, that store also handles your data under its own privacy policy (see section 3.8).

3. What the game processes

3.1 On your device only

The game keeps these things on your device, and they are not sent to us:

  • your settings;
  • your in-game name;
  • whether you allowed statistics;
  • the players you muted;
  • replays of your matches, which contain the names the players used;
  • player reports that are waiting for a connection.

When you play against the computer, in the unit lab or on a local network (LAN), the game data goes only between your device and the devices on your local network. Nothing goes to us. You can remove all of it by clearing the game's data or uninstalling the game.

3.2 Online play

Online matches use Unity Gaming Services, run by Unity Technologies on our behalf (see section 5). When you choose to play online, the game does four things:

  • Signs in anonymously. Unity creates a random player ID for your installation and keeps a sign-in token on your device. There is no e-mail and no password.
  • Creates or joins a match session. Unity's servers keep the following for as long as the session lasts:
    • your player ID;
    • your in-game name, which also names a match you host;
    • your seat, faction, colour and ready state;
    • the match settings and the game version.
    The other players in the session see your name and your choices.
  • Finds the closest relay region. The game measures which of Unity's relay regions is closest to you.
  • Connects you to the other players through Unity's Relay servers, with encryption. To do that, Unity's servers process your IP address while you are connected. We don't receive or store it.

Legal basis: performance of the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Online play can't work without the player ID and the connection. If you don't want this processing, you can still play alone or on a local network.

3.3 Chat

Messages you write in the lobby or in a match go to the other players of that match. They travel through the game of the player who hosts the match and through Unity's relay. The chat is not saved by us or on a server. It only lives in the players' games during the match, except when someone reports a player (section 3.4). Please don't write personal information in the chat: the other players can read it.

3.4 Muting and reporting players

You can mute any player. The list of players you muted stays on your device. You can also report a player who breaks the rules shown in the game. A report reaches us through Unity's User Reporting service and contains:

  • the reported player's in-game name and player ID;
  • the reason you chose;
  • the reported player's chat messages from that match;
  • your player ID;
  • the game version and platform;
  • the match ID and the time;
  • a technical description of your device that the service adds: model, operating system, graphics and memory;
  • the game's last log lines. These are technical messages, which can contain the names of the players in that match.

Reports contain no screenshots. A report made without a connection waits on your device and is sent the next time the game connects.

We read every report ourselves; no decision is made automatically. We use reports only to keep online play safe and to enforce its rules, for example by excluding a player who breaks them from online play.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest, and the other players', in safe and fair online play (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). App stores also require games with chat to let players report abuse. If you were reported, you have the same rights over that data as everyone else (section 8).

3.5 Statistics, only with your consent

On its first start the game asks whether it may collect anonymous statistics. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy.

On the date of this policy the statistics service is not connected, so nothing is sent, even if you allowed it. Before we switch it on, we will update this policy with exactly what is collected. It will be limited to match and technical data linked to a random ID, never to your name or your contacts. Examples: the faction played, the match length and result, the game version, the device type and crashes.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with the same switch.

3.6 The Unity engine

Desert Clash is built with the Unity engine. The engine itself sends Unity Technologies some technical information about your device and the engine. Examples: the device model, the operating system, the graphics hardware and the engine version. Unity uses this information for its own purposes, to understand how its software is used and to improve it. We don't receive this data. Unity handles it under its own Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, and you can contact Unity about it at DPO@unity3d.com.

To the extent we are responsible for this transmission, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in building the game with a widely used engine (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3.7 Purchases and ads

The current version shows no ads and has no real purchases. In test versions the faction shop unlocks factions for free, and nothing is charged. If we add purchases or ads, we will update this policy before the version that has them.

3.8 App stores

When you install the game from Google Play (or, later, Steam), the store processes your account, download and purchase data as its own controller, under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy. Google Play may also give us crash and performance reports from users who allowed Google to collect them. These reports are aggregated or technical and don't tell us who you are. If you take part in a test of the game, we keep the e-mail address of the Google account you gave us, for the list of testers. Legal basis: your request to take part (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. This website

  • Hosting. The website is static, hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. Like any web server, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the pages and to keep the service secure, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Videos. The videos come from YouTube only after you press play. Until then nothing is requested from YouTube. After you press play, YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and may store data on your device under Google's privacy policy. The player is loaded from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • E-mails. If you write to us, we use your e-mail address and your message to answer you. We delete them at the latest 2 years after the conversation ends, unless we need them longer to handle a legal claim. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you, or steps you asked for (Art. 6(1)(f) and (b) GDPR).

5. Who receives the data

  • Unity Technologies processes the online play and report data on our behalf, as our processor, under Unity's data processing terms. This covers Unity Authentication, Sessions (Lobby), Relay and User Reporting.
  • The other players in your online match see your in-game name, your messages and your choices in that match.
  • Google, Unity and GitHub receive data for their own purposes, as described in sections 3.6, 3.8 and 4.
  • Authorities receive data only if the law requires it.

We don't sell personal data, don't share it for advertising, and don't use it for profiling.

6. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Unity Technologies, Google and GitHub are based in the United States, and data may be processed there and in other countries. These transfers rely on the safeguards recognised by the GDPR: the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, where the company is certified under it, or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected;
  • have it deleted;
  • restrict its processing;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

Write to twogreenpigs.studio@gmail.com. We answer within one month. Because you play without an account, we don't know who you are. To find your data, we may ask for the in-game name you used and roughly when and where you played. We can only act on data we can link to you.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Romania this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP, www.dataprotection.ro). You can also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

9. Children

Desert Clash is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it. In the European Union, players under 16 may allow the optional statistics only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

10. Security

The game's online data is encrypted on its way to Unity's services and to the other players. Access to the data on Unity's dashboard is limited to us.

11. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date. If a change needs your consent, the game asks for it again. If Two Green Pigs becomes a registered company, that company will take over as controller, and this policy will say so.
