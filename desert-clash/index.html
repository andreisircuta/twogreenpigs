How it plays

Build. Place. Watch it march.

Your grid is your army

You never order your soldiers around. You decide what to build, where to place it on your grid and when to invest. Tanks in front, archers behind, casters in the middle. Formation matters: spread your bombers, bait their splash, hide your healers.

Waves, not clicks

Every minute your whole grid marches out as a wave, keeps its formation and fights its way down a single lane towards the enemy citadel. Units fight on their own, with their own spells: storms, frost novas, cannons, healing, raising the dead. They come back every wave, so every choice keeps paying off, or keeps costing you.

Counter the enemy

Scout their grid, then answer. Every unit has a counter and every army has a weakness: the match is won by reading the other side and adapting your army wave after wave.

Four factions

Four factions that play nothing alike

The Horde

Orcs, goblins and beasts. Cheap swarms, charging grunts, ogres, wyverns and dragons.

The Kingdom

Steel and gunpowder. Crossbows and muskets, catapults, steam golems, war balloons and flying galleons with a Great Cannon.

The Arcane Order

Elven magic. Shields that recharge, blinking archers, crystal striders that sweep the field with fire, storms of arcane lightning.

The Nightblood

The undead. Zombie hordes, liches that raise your fallen as their own, bone colossi and swarms that latch onto their prey.

More than 60 units, each with its own look, weapon and role.

Teamwork

Win as a team

Three players per side, each with a grid and a wave of their own. Hold the middle to raise your whole team's income, break their tower for a bounty, tech up, build mines and upgrade your weapons and armour. Then break their citadel.

Features

What's in the box

  • 3v3 matches: you and two allies against three.
  • Online play with friends. Cross-play between PC and Android is planned.
  • AI allies and opponents on four difficulties, from relaxed to merciless.
  • Upgrades that change units: tiers, weapon and armour levels, cloaking, blinking, the Great Cannon.
  • Economy decisions: mines, the middle bonus, the tower bounty.
  • Replays of every match.
  • Stylized low-poly art, full of colour and effects.
  • 21 languages.
